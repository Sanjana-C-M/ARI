Health claims and Science myths keep spreading on Social Medias after fact-checkers have debunked them. Most fact-checks are written in English or Hindi, so the Tamil speakers who receive these forwards rarely see them. People who finds typing or English search hard have no quick way to check a claim in their own language.

Tamil heritage's literature, festivals, and history are central to Tamil identity, but the easiest sources to find are often in English, unsourced, or unreliable. Young people and parents have little to check against when they want to learn about the Sangam poetry, or a festival such as Aadi Perukku.

So I built Ari.

Ari is a voice app in Tamil. A user asks a question out loud, or types it, and Ari replies with a short, checked answer in Tamil, shows its source, and plays it in a Tamil voice. If it has no checked answer, it says “I don’t know” instead of guessing.

Myths: “Does heating food in a microwave make it radioactive?” Ari checks it and says no.

Heritage: “What is Aadi Perukku?” Ari explains it is a festival that gives thanks for the new floodwater in the rivers.

Most AI chatbots generate what sounds right. Ari only answers from checked entries, so every answer comes with its source.

What makes it different from a general AI chatbot
---It answers only from checked entries, not from a guess.
---Every answer shows its source.
---It refuses rather than guesses, including on disputed topics.
---The same question always gives the same checked answer.
